Automatic strike damage, spell saves and spell damage now reach creatures on any scene. The GM no longer has to open the map a fight is on: the roll deck aims PF2e's own apply and save controls at the target's token document for one click, instead of borrowing the GM's canvas selection, so a hit on another scene lands straight away. The strike formula check also never falls back to whatever the GM happens to have targeted.
