Health bands posted to the combat topic can now say what hit the creature ("Turret Disintegrator from Changer") and carry the attacker's and the creature's token pictures as a two-photo album. The weapon and attacker are named only when players can see the attacker: not hidden, not invisible or undetected, and with a name players can read. The creature's picture is sent only when players can see its name. Resistances and weaknesses stay in the GM's message.
