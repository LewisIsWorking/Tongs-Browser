Each Take a Breather (No-Limit Stamina) is now posted to the character's campaign's Telegram combat topic, in or out of combat: the GM's browser sends the Stamina regained and that no Resolve Point was spent. The campaign is the one whose party lists the character, so a world shared by two campaigns posts to the right one. It needs a GM signed in to ComeOnOverUno, and a ComeOnOverUno server with the new breather endpoint.
