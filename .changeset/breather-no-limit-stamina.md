Take a Breather (No-Limit Stamina), the Gamemastery Guide's option for the Stamina variant: in a Stamina world, Tongs adds a "Take a Breather (No-Limit Stamina)" macro to the Macro Directory once. A player selects their token (or just uses their assigned character), runs it, rests 10 minutes out of combat and has their Stamina Points refilled to max, with no Resolve Point needed or spent. The chat card names No-Limit Stamina. Worlds without the Stamina variant are told it does nothing there, and a character in a started encounter is skipped.
