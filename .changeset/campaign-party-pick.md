Where two campaigns share a world (C00 and C01, C06 and C11), a new player's sheet now goes in the party of the campaign they post in. ComeOnOverUno tells the GM's browser (and its helper GM, through `helper.campaigns(map)`) which campaigns each player posts in, and Tongs keeps it in a hidden world setting. The welcome offers a player only their own campaign's party, and a loose sheet joins its player's campaign party. A player in both campaigns, or in neither, is still asked.
