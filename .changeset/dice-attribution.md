Aimed and sneaking hits are applied automatically again. A hit was checked by rolling the strike a second time and demanding the same formula, which cannot work for damage that depends on the target: an operative's Aim die and Sneak Attack's precision die are worked out from the fight as it was when the player rolled. Tongs now checks that every die on the card is one PF2e named, alongside the paired hit, the once-only rule and a total inside the roll's own range.
