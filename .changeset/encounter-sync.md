Encounter sync: with the new "Sync encounters to the combat topic" setting on, the GM's browser keeps one tracker message per Foundry encounter in the campaign's Telegram combat topic, through ComeOnOverUno: the round, the allies still to act with their players named and those who have acted struck off, then the enemies. Hidden and defeated combatants are left out and enemies are named as players may see them. A new "Telegram players" menu links each Foundry player to their Path Wars Telegram player. The Path Wars bot reads the encounter from ComeOnOverUno to ping.
