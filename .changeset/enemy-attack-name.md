Only a player's attack is named in public. When an enemy, hazard or other GM-run creature hurts or heals someone, the combat topic and the HP record name the creature alone ("takes 2 from Skeleton Infantry."), not its attack. A player's attack or spell is still named, persistent damage is still named by its condition, and the GM's DM still gets the full cause.
