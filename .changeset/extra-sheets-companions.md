'tongs-browser': minor
---

A player ComeOnOverUno grants an extra sheet (the bot's `/extrasheet`) is offered a second character sheet, which joins their campaign's party; nobody can have more than two, and the GM's browser now enforces that cap rather than trusting the welcome. Characters with a familiar, animal companion, eidolon, undead or construct companion (found by PF2e's feat and class feature slugs) automatically get a companion sheet, owned by their player, in their party, made once; companion sheets never count toward the cap. Tongs assumes the PF2e Companion Compendia module is active and tells the player which of its packs to drag the ancestry from.

Users ComeOnOverUno lists in `partyOwners` are made owners of the world's primary party sheet (PF2e's active party, never any other party), so they can open the party stash; nobody's ownership is ever lowered.
