When ComeOnOverUno stops accepting Tongs's sign-in, the GM now gets a lasting notice saying hits, health bands and the encounter tracker are not reaching Telegram, and how to sign in again. Before, the sign-in was dropped without a word, which is how a whole C06 fight's damage went unposted. A server error while ComeOnOverUno restarts no longer drops the sign-in at all.
