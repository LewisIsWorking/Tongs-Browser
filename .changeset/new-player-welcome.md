New players are welcomed. A player with no character sheet is asked for a name, and a GM's browser makes the sheet in the campaign's party (the party with a campaign code), owned by them and assigned as their character. With no GM online the request waits and is made the moment one joins. The new sheet then opens with a checklist: ancestry, heritage, background, class, attribute boosts and equipment, ticked off as they are filled in. A world setting, "Welcome new players", switches it off.
