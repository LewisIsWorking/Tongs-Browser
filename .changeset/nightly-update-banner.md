ComeOnOverUno's nightly Foundry update now closes the open world at 06:00 UK time instead of skipping every night a world was open. Five minutes before, everyone in the world sees a countdown banner; when the world closes, Tongs takes them to COO's progress page, which shows each step of the update and sends them back when the same world is open again.
