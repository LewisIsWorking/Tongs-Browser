Health bands now follow each party's campaign, so one world can host several Path Wars campaigns.

The world-wide **Path Wars campaign for health bands** setting is gone. Instead, **Party campaigns** in the
module settings gives each party its own campaign code, such as C06. When an enemy is hurt, its band goes
to the campaign of the player characters fighting in that combat.

- A combat with no coded party, or with characters from two different campaigns, posts nothing, and the
  GM is told why once, so a band never lands in another table's topic.
- A value that is not a campaign code (a C and digits) is refused rather than saved.
