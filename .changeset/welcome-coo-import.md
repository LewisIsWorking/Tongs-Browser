New players can start from a ComeOnOverUno character. On the welcome, choose a "Foundry VTT" export from the PF2e Character Creator or Viewer; once a GM's browser makes the sheet, the player's own browser fills it from PF2e's compendiums (ancestry, heritage, background, class, feats and lore), sets the attribute modifiers and level, and asks them any choices PF2e needs. Anything it cannot find, and any spells, are listed for them to add by hand.
